# Glossary

**Section**
A group of cards under one header in Moxfield's deck view, as determined by the active "Group by" mode (e.g. Creatures under Type grouping, a custom tag under Category grouping). Also called a *category* by users; *Section* is canonical.

**Board**
One of Moxfield's top-level card pools: Mainboard, Commander, Companion, Sideboard, Maybeboard, Considering, Tokens, plus format-specific ones such as Signature Spell, Attraction Deck and Contraption Deck. Sections live inside a board. Only the Mainboard feeds the Library.

**Library**
The cards that start in the library: every mainboard card, counting quantities, excluding commanders (which start in the command zone). The population for Section odds.

**Section odds**
The probability of having seen at least the desired number of a Section's cards by a given turn, drawing from the Library. Only mainboard Sections have Section odds.

**Mana value**
A card's rules-defined total cost: X counts as 0, a split card is both halves combined, and an MDFC, transforming card or adventure card counts its front or main face.
_Avoid_: CMC, converted mana cost, casting cost

**Section mana curve**
A small bar graph in a Section header showing how many of the Section's cards (counting quantities) sit at each mana value. Cards with no mana cost, such as lands, are left out.
_Avoid_: mana curve overlay, sparkline
